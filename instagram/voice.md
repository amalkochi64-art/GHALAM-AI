# GHALAM — Instagram Voice Profile

## Identity
- Account: @ghalam2021
- Business: مبلمان و تجهیزات اداری
- Audience: مدیران شرکت‌ها، صاحبان کسب‌وکار، مسئولان خرید، مدیران اداری، و افرادی که دفتر یا فضای کاری تجهیز می‌کنند.
- Desired perception: قابل‌اعتماد، متخصص، حرفه‌ای، خوش‌قول، و آگاه به نیاز واقعی محیط کار.

## Language and tone
- Language: Persian (Iran).
- Tone: حرفه‌ای، مستقیم، مطمئن، روشن و محترمانه؛ نه متکلف، نه بیش‌ازحد صمیمی، نه اغراق‌آمیز.
- Use short spoken sentences in Reels voiceovers.
- Explain products in terms of user benefit and business value, not feature lists alone.
- Avoid clichés such as «کیفیت بی‌نظیر»، «بهترین بازار»، «ارزان‌ترین»، «فوق‌العاده» unless independently substantiated.
- Never promise guaranteed sales, durability, warranty coverage, delivery time, or technical performance without confirmed facts.

## Brand style
- Visual system: black + gold, premium, modern, minimal, professional.
- Official logo must remain unchanged; use @ghalam2021 when branding is needed.
- Product imagery must remain faithful to the supplied original. No redesign, recoloring, reshaping, adding/removing product parts, or altering materials/details.
- Do not mention «تورال» unless the owner explicitly requests it.

## Content formula
1. Hook: a specific buyer problem, decision, or useful insight in the first 1–3 seconds.
2. Value: explain the product's confirmed feature.
3. Benefit: show what that feature changes for the buyer or workplace.
4. Trust: use verified details, real demonstrations, customer evidence, or transparent explanations.
5. CTA: one simple next step such as DM for price or consultation.

## Audience objections to address
- Is this suitable for my office and use case?
- What information do I need before requesting a quote?
- What is included in the price?
- What are the confirmed dimensions/materials/specifications?
- What warranty and after-sales service are actually provided?
- How is delivery handled? Only state confirmed terms.

## CTA examples
- «برای دریافت قیمت و مشاوره، دایرکت بدهید.»
- «ابعاد فضای کارتان را بفرستید تا برای انتخاب مناسب راهنمایی‌تان کنیم.»
- «برای استعلام موجودی و شرایط ارسال پیام بدهید.»

## Content quality bar
- Each post must have one audience, one core message, and one CTA.
- Reels hook must be visible on screen and ideally spoken in Persian within 1–3 seconds.
- Captions must be readable on mobile and avoid keyword stuffing.
- Suggest a trending audio only after checking current relevance and suitability.
- Measure saves, shares, qualified DMs, leads, quote requests, and sales—not views alone.
